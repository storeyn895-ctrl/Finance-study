RESERVE BANK OF NEW ZEALAND — TE PŪTEA MATUA
OFFICIAL CASH RATE SUMMARY AND RECORD
19 February 2025

The Monetary Policy Committee today agreed to reduce the Official Cash Rate (OCR) by 50 basis points, from 4.25% to 3.75%.

Annual consumer price inflation returned to within the Monetary Policy Committee's 1 to 3 percent target band in the second half of 2024. Inflation expectations are settling near the 2 percent midpoint.

The New Zealand economy is recovering from a period of below-potential growth. Productive capacity now exceeds spending and output. There is spare productive capacity in the economy.

Global economic growth is expected to remain subdued. If global trade barriers increase significantly, New Zealand's export growth and broader economic activity could be adversely affected.

The Committee has scope to lower the OCR further as inflation remains near target. The pace of further easing will depend on the Committee's confidence that pricing behaviour remains consistent with low and stable inflation.

Source: Reserve Bank of New Zealand, February 2025 OCR Review
